// Each rune grants two spells (see PHB2024_Rune_Unlock_* in Passive.txt):
// - The rune spell below: the free 1/long rest cast, paid with the rune's charge.
// - The vanilla spell, for casting with spell slots. Slot casting is left to the
//   vanilla spell so the engine handles SpellSlotsGroup costs (normal and Warlock
//   slots) and upcasting through its own _2.._N variants.

// SpellSlot resources, all showed as level 1, and could not be upcast properly.
